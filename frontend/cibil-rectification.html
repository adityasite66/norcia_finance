Our Process

How CIBIL rectification assistance works

We first understand the issue visible in the customer's CIBIL report and then guide the rectification process.

01 — Check Report We review the customer's CIBIL report to identify the issue.
02 — Identify Discrepancy If an incorrect loan or incorrect reporting is identified, the case can be taken up for rectification assistance.
03 — Rectification Process We assist with the process of getting eligible incorrect information corrected.
Processing Time: The CIBIL rectification process takes at least 30 days.
